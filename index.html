The Void Crushers

You are a Master Geneticist in a distant future where the multiverse is collapsing under the weight of The Void. Evil Bosses representing entropy and decay stand between your civilization and survival.

Using DNA extracted from fallen foes, you synthesize, mutate, and evolve a roster of Beasts—living weapons designed to exploit elemental weaknesses, scale synergistically, and crush the Void Bosses.

Gameplay Guide

  • DNA & Gold: Defeating Bosses earns Gold for the run. Failing to kill a boss earns persistent DNA.
  • Laboratory: Spend DNA here to permanently upgrade your evolutionary tech tree across all runs.
  • Compute & Execute: Uses a Genetic Algorithm to find the optimal arrangement of beasts from your inventory based on their synergies, elemental affinities, and the Boss's current stance.
  • Epochs: More epochs = more time for the GA to mutate and crossover sequences, finding higher damage combinations.
